# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Snippedia to be a welcoming, respectful project for people exploring education, product design, and frontend engineering.

Contributors are expected to be kind, constructive, and collaborative. Disagreement is fine; harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or intentionally disruptive behavior are not.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give feedback that is specific, useful, and focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when they happen.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Repeated disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests
- Any conduct that would reasonably make contributors feel unsafe

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.
